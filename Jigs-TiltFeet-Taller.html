Taller Tilt Feet

Overview

Taller tilt foot

Dan Heil has designed taller feet for the Tormk. Two of these can be used on the left side of the machine to tilt the whole machine in order to route water from the cutting stone to the right and more effectively into the water tray when sharpening various tools. More details are available at the Thinigiverse page.

One set is 2x the height of the standard TF-22 Tilt Feet; the other is 3x the height.

Notes on Making One

The design can be found on Thingiverse.com at Triple_Height_Tormek_Riser_foot.
