Printable Handbook

The Sharpening Handbook, 2026 Edition, is the content of this web site laid out as a book to print at home. Each chapter is its own PDF, so you can print just the chapters you need and keep them in a 3-ring binder. Page numbers include the chapter number (e.g., 6-14), so a chapter can be replaced when it is updated without reprinting the others. Each chapter’s first page shows the date it was last revised.

Choose Letter (8½" × 11") or A4. The pages are laid out for printing on both sides, with a wider margin on the side for the binder holes.
